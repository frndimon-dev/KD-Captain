Section 2: Battery Care & Charging Protocol

Authorized Charging Only: I agree to charge the EV scooter using only the official OEM charger provided by KD Captain. I will strictly avoid third-party, modified, or unauthorized charging points.

No Extreme Discharge: I will not let the battery drop below 15–20% State of Charge (SoC) to prevent deep discharge degradation.

Temperature & Water Safety: I will never charge the battery immediately after high-speed or heavy-load riding (will allow 15–30 minutes cooldown) and will never plug in charging connectors when wet or exposed to heavy rain/waterlogging.

Tampering Prohibition: I will not attempt to open, bypass, modify, or repair the battery pack, charging socket, or onboard wiring. Any unauthorized tampering will lead to immediate confiscation and financial liability for replacement costs.

Section 3: Vehicle Maintenance & Physical Safety

Daily Pre-Ride Check: I am responsible for inspecting tire pressure, brake responsiveness, headlights, indicators, and rearview mirrors before starting my daily shift.

Weight & Passenger Restrictions: I understand that the scooter is strictly rated for a maximum payload (rider + cargo not exceeding manufacturer limit, typically 130–150 kg). Overloading or unauthorized triple riding is strictly prohibited.

Waterlogged Roads: During Kolkata monsoons, I will not ride the EV through water submerged above wheel-hub level to protect the hub motor and controller.

Damage Reporting & Routine Servicing: Any mechanical failure, flat tire, or structural damage must be reported via the KD Captain support channel within 2 hours. I agree to bring the vehicle to designated service points on scheduled dates.

Section 6: Rental Payments & Deposit Terms

On-Time Settlement: Daily/weekly/monthly rental fees must be cleared before the agreed cutoff time. Failure to pay on time will result in remote vehicle immobilization via the telematics system.

Security Deposit Deduction: KD Captain reserves the right to deduct costs from the security deposit for unpaid fines, battery damages due to negligence, physical dents, or unpaid daily rental balances.

Section 7: Advanced Battery Care & Electrical Safeguards

Approved Power Sockets Only: I will only plug the OEM charger into a standard, properly grounded 3-pin 16A/6A power outlet. Direct wire tapping or loose sockets are strictly prohibited.

Thermal Management & Overnight Charging: I will never charge the battery in direct sunlight during extreme summer heat or leave the battery connected continuously for more than 8 hours.

Zero Drop Rule: For detachable batteries, I will carry them using designated handles with both hands. Continuing to charge or use a dropped/dented battery is strictly forbidden due to fire hazard.

No Unofficial Fast Chargers: Any battery degradation or burnout caused by unofficial charging will be billed to me at 100% replacement cost.

Section 8: Prevention of Fleet Mishandling, Stunts & Abuse

Zero Reckless Driving: The scooter is strictly for utility and legal delivery purposes. Performing stunts, wheelies, aggressive braking, or racing is strictly banned.

Cargo & Modifications: The footboard and luggage rack must only carry properly secured parcel bags. Transporting sharp construction materials or gas cylinders is prohibited. No unauthorized electrical modifications are allowed.

Tire, Rim Protection & Washing: Riding on a flat tire is prohibited. Cleaning must be done with a damp cloth only; high-pressure jet washing on the battery and console is prohibited.

Section 9: Strict Legal Prohibitions, Contraband & Crime Compliance

Absolute Contraband Prohibition (NDPS & Arms Act): Under Section 60(3) of the NDPS Act and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), I declare that I will NEVER use the vehicle to carry, transport, conceal, or deliver narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, illegal alcohol, smuggled goods, weapons, explosives, or banned products.

No Criminal Involvement & Zero-Connivance Indemnity: If the vehicle is impounded or seized due to illegal acts committed by me, I am fully liable for all legal costs, fines, and the complete commercial value of the scooter, facing immediate criminal prosecution.

Commercial Transport Documentation: I will ensure all deliveries carry valid merchant bills, delivery platform digital manifests, or e-way bills.

Section 10: Responsible Delivery, Customer Conduct & Public Safety

Safe Cargo & No Phone Distraction: Cargo must not obstruct handlebar movement or rider visibility. Riding while holding a phone in hand or wearing noise-canceling headphones is prohibited.

Pedestrian & Traffic Courtesy: I will strictly respect pedestrian walkways and traffic rules. Any road rage or abusive behavior will lead to blacklisting and retrieval of the scooter.

Section 11: Geofencing, Telematics & Vehicle Inspection

Geographic Boundary Compliance: The scooter is authorized strictly within the Kolkata Metropolitan Area (KMA). Taking it outside triggers automatic remote motor shutdown.

Right to Spot Inspection & Immediate Surrender: KD Captain officials have the right to inspect the vehicle at any time. Upon surrender notice, I agree to return the vehicle within 4 hours with all dues cleared.
